Hariclia Grammenou

Joseph Rutland

 

Hariclia's Backstage

Hariclia is an actor originally from Greece, now based in Liverpool, where she moved to follow her passion for performing. She holds a BA (Hons) in Performing Arts (Acting) from The City of Liverpool College, and she continues to develop her craft through regular workshops and by taking part in local short film projects. She has worked on multiple short films with some incredible local directors including myself such as The Magic Spellbook and Head Splitting. She's also worked quite a bit in theatre, mainly with The Little Theatre in the Wirral on plays such as Fleet Street Nativity and Agatha Christie’s The Mirror Crack’d. Though she focuses on theatre quite a lot, her greatest passion is film and she has been working hard on meeting great talent to collaborate with.
