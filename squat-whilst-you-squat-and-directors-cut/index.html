Squat Whilst You Squat (And Director's Cut)

Joseph Rutland

God, this one was a nightmare. Another result of my film challange, I received the following prompt from Ross McCartan "A man comes home to find a woman is squatting in his home. He tries reasoning with her but she has some outrageous demands. Eventually reasoning turns into a conversation and the two seemingly connect until she finally decides to let the man back into his home... Only for her to kick him back out again after they spend the night together."

My dad helped me with the gym idea and very shortly I shipped the script off to the director who was supposed to film it. Weeks went by during the filming segment with the director for this film asking for extra time every week until she asked me to do all the casting and location scouting for her, which I happily obliged. All was going well until  the director was unable to come to set on the day, so I was put in charge. Then it took nearly a year for it to be edited, after which I decided to make my own cut because I was bored. There was also a broken bycycle that none of the cast trusted, which I balanced some camera gear on a road for a few miles.
