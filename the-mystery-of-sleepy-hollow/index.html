The Mystery of Sleepy Hollow

Joseph Rutland

A very spooky mystery from the mind of Elliot Kinear. Now, I know what you may be thinking: Isn't sleepy hollow already a thing? Yes, this is the unofficial sequel set 10 years after the original. I played Brouwer (who I belive was in the OG version) and I was joined by other OG characters and various epic brand new characters whose names are very difficult to pronounce like 'Marshall Kneijnsberg'.

Tell you what though, it was a banging good production. Very well written, a tight script and a mystery that I wouldn't have guessed but my parents did infact guess.
