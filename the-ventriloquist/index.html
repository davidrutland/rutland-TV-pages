The Ventriloquist

Joseph Rutland

Possibly my favourite of my own films. The film evolved from the idea of somebody kidnapping a guy at a bar and slowly replacing his limbs with wooden ones until he became a puppet. Finding all the extras for the final scene was an absolute nightmare for me, too.

The sections outside spoons were shot with a DJI mini, and the rest with a regular C100. First time working with Josh Whittington too! I actually bumped into my aunt and her freinds when shooting some scenes with the Ventriloquist however the photos from this encounter are unfortunately not in Joseph's possession yet.
