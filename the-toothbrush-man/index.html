The Toothbrush Man

Joseph Rutland

This was my latest 48 hour film challange contribution! I had the pleasure of reuniting with Sam Bolton who I worked on The Ketamine film with. It started with us getting toothbrush a prop we had use and 'I can't hear you!' as the line we had to include.

Callum helpfully allowed us use of his street and his bathroom. I recall me ranting about this 'The Toothbrush Man' creature. I kinda wanted him to have a tooth hat like the guards from the film 'The Hogfather'. One very big concern was making sure it didn't look like it was a superhero. It's just The Toothbrush man, like The Mothman or The Lockness Monster.
